Atlas is where I experiment with the architecture of my systems before taking it into a real product. It works as a boilerplate: a modular base I can start new projects on without rebuilding the same things every time.

## What I'm focusing on

- Modular architecture: every part of the system can be added, removed or replaced without breaking the rest.
- Extensibility and reuse across products.
- Patterns for business systems, such as multi-tenancy.

## What has come out of it

The concepts I validate in Atlas are the foundation of [Café del Tiempo](/en/projects/cafe-del-tiempo/).
