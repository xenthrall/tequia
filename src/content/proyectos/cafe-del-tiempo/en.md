Café del Tiempo is a personal suite anyone can host on their own server. It's built on the foundation and concepts I develop in [Atlas](/en/projects/atlas/).

## Modules

- **Vault:** credentials and secrets with client-side encryption (zero-knowledge). The server never sees your data in plain text.
- **Finance:** income, expenses and monthly reports.

It's open source under the MIT license.
